This is a guide to weekend activities in Bangalore.

## 1. Escape rooms

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: About two hours.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/01-escape-room.webp" alt="A dim brick-walled escape room lit by a wall lamp" />

Escape rooms are a great experience for intellectually driven friend groups. Participants are locked in a room filled with clues that need to be deciphered. It is definitely a must-have experience for anyone who has never tried an escape room.

## 2. Bangalore Palace

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: About two hours.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/02-bangalore-palace.webp" alt="Bangalore Palace beneath a blue sky" />

A visit to Bangalore Palace offers a glimpse of how kings used to live. It is nowhere comparable to Mysore Palace, but it is worth visiting at least once while in the city. I especially like the audio guide they have.

## 3. Visvesvaraya Museum

<IconList icon="clock">
- Best time: Morning.
- Duration: About four hours, though covering every experiment may take a full day.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/03-visvesvaraya-museum.webp" alt="A large mechanical exhibit inside Visvesvaraya Industrial and Technological Museum" />

Visvesvaraya Museum is a good place for anyone who was interested in science while growing up. It has a lot of scientific experiments, including my favourite light-splitting experiment.

## 4. Movies

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: About four hours.
</IconList>

<HalftoneImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/04-movies.webp" alt="Rows of red seats facing a cinema screen" />

Going to the movies with friends is an obvious but great experience. In my opinion, horror movies are a good choice.

A movie may not involve much active time together, but it is still worth doing.

## 5. IKEA

<IconList icon="clock">
- Best time: From its opening at 11:00.
- Duration: Until around 4:00; it can be a whole-day event.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/05-ikea.webp" alt="A furnished living-room display inside IKEA" />

IKEA might sound like an unusual choice, but it is a good place to spend quality time with a friend or a date. The furnished rooms can be explored, and there are places to sit, talk, and have some really good food. Their Swedish meatballs are too good. IKEA is insanely big.

## 6. National Gallery of Modern Art

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: About three hours.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/06-modern-art-gallery.webp" alt="Black-and-white portrait artworks displayed at the National Gallery of Modern Art" />

This gallery is decent and has some very interesting art. It is a no-brainer for art lovers.

## 7. Clubbing

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: An entire night.
</IconList>

<HalftoneImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/07-clubbing.webp" alt="A DJ performing for a crowded nightclub dance floor" />

Bangalore has a lot of places for clubbing and partying, including Koramangala and Indiranagar. Koramangala is more suitable for dancing and dance floors, while Indiranagar has many resto-bars and is better suited to sitting down and talking over a beer. Bob's Bar is one option that is definitely worth visiting at least once.

## 8. Food and cafes

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: A couple of hours.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/08-food-and-cafes.webp" alt="Tables and wicker chairs in a plant-filled outdoor cafe" />

Bangalore is filled with great restaurants offering different cuisines, making them good places for a weekend meal with friends. Truffles is one option. Bangalore also has many new cafes with overpriced coffee and below-par food. Almost all have a great ambience, but very few have good food.

## 9. Playing board games with friends

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: Up to an entire day.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/09-board-games.webp" alt="A collection of classic board-game boxes" />

A good collection of board games offers one of the best ways to enjoy a weekend with friends. Some options are Catan and Monopoly.

## 10. Parks like Cubbon Park and Lalbagh

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: One to two hours.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/10-cubbon-park-lalbagh.webp" alt="A shaded path beneath large green trees in a Bangalore park" />

Cubbon Park and Lalbagh are the go-to places for spending time walking in nature.

## 11. Stand-up comedy

<IconList icon="clock">
- Best time: Weekends.
- Duration: One and a half to two hours.
</IconList>

<HalftoneImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/11-stand-up-comedy.webp" alt="A stand-up comedian performing with a microphone" />

Bangalore's comedy scene is also quite good. Interesting shows take place throughout the week and are worth attending when a specific comedian is of interest. There are also comedy nights featuring a lineup of comedians, though they can be hit or miss depending on the lineup. Tickets usually cost around 200 or 300 rupees.

## 12. Wonderla

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: A full day.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/12-wonderla.webp" alt="A water ride surrounded by greenery at Wonderla" />

Wonderla is an amusement park close to Bangalore and is worth visiting with a group of friends. It has both water and land rides.

## 13. Plays

<IconList icon="clock">
- Duration: About two hours.
</IconList>

<PaperTextureImage src="/posts/29072026_bangaloreweekend/images/13-plays.webp" alt="Actors performing a play on stage" />

Different plays are often listed on BookMyShow. They can sometimes be hit or miss, but watching a play and seeing drama performed live is an experience worth having.
